# Type 2 Diabetes Care Pathway (CP-DM2-001)

> **Fictional training document.** Synthetic content for a workshop. Not clinical guidance.

## DM2-1 Scope

DM2-1.1 Applies to adults with type 2 diabetes admitted after a hyperglycaemia episode.

DM2-1.2 Ward of reference: Endocrinology 3C.

## DM2-2 Discharge coordination checklist

DM2-2.1 Diabetes specialist nurse education session completed (glucose monitoring, sick-day
guidance as provided by the clinical team).

DM2-2.2 Glucose meter or continuous glucose monitor arranged. A continuous glucose monitor
(`ENDO-CGM`) requires a prior-authorization check (PA-001).

DM2-2.3 Medication reconciliation completed (MED-001). Combinations such as prednisolone with
insulin glargine are flagged by the synthetic rule set (MED-001 §MED-4) and must be reviewed by a
pharmacist — the coordinator does not change doses.

## DM2-3 Follow-up requirements

| Follow-up | Deadline | Reference |
|---|---|---|
| Endocrinology clinic review | within **14 days** of discharge | FU-001 §FU-3 |
| Primary care review | within **14 days** of discharge | FU-001 §FU-3 |
| Retinal screening check | confirm date on record | DM2-4 |

## DM2-4 Long-term care reminders

DM2-4.1 Confirm the patient's annual retinal screening and foot check dates are recorded with
primary care. Missing dates are flagged in the discharge summary, not booked by the coordinator.

## DM2-5 Devices and procedures

| Procedure code | Description |
|---|---|
| `ENDO-CGM` | Continuous glucose monitor (sensor starter pack) |
| `ENDO-PUMP` | Insulin pump |

## DM2-6 Escalation

DM2-6.1 Reports of very high or very low glucose readings with symptoms (confusion, vomiting,
drowsiness) are emergencies — escalate immediately (SAFE-001 §SAFE-ESC-01).
