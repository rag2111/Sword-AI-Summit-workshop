# Heart Failure Care Pathway (CP-HF-001)

> **Fictional training document.** Synthetic content for a workshop. Not clinical guidance.

## HF-1 Scope

HF-1.1 Applies to adults admitted with heart failure, including heart failure with reduced
ejection fraction (HFrEF) and a new diagnosis of heart failure.

HF-1.2 Ward of reference: Cardiology 4B.

## HF-2 Discharge criteria (coordination checklist)

HF-2.1 The treating cardiologist has documented clinical stability. Coordinators do not assess
clinical stability themselves.

HF-2.2 Daily-weight diary and scales arranged for home.

HF-2.3 Medication reconciliation completed (MED-001), with particular attention to combinations
flagged by the network's synthetic interaction rule set (for example spironolactone with
lisinopril, see MED-001 §MED-4).

## HF-3 Follow-up requirements

| Follow-up | Deadline | Reference |
|---|---|---|
| Cardiology clinic review | within **7 days** of discharge | FU-001 §FU-3 |
| Primary care review | within **14 days** of discharge | FU-001 §FU-3 |
| Heart failure nurse phone call | within **3 days** of discharge | HF-3.3 |

HF-3.1 The cardiology appointment must be **booked before discharge** (DP-001 §DP-3.3). If no
cardiology slot is available within 7 days, the coordinator escalates to the Heart Failure Clinic
lead (SAFE-001 §SAFE-ESC-05) — the deadline must not be silently extended.

HF-3.2 For a **new diagnosis** of heart failure, an echocardiogram (procedure code `CARD-ECHO`)
is usually requested. Check prior-authorization requirements with the patient's payer (PA-001).

HF-3.3 The heart failure nurse phone call is arranged by the ward and is out of scope for the
digital assistant.

## HF-4 Patient education topics

- Recognising worsening symptoms (weight gain of more than 2 kg in 3 days, breathlessness).
- Fluid and salt guidance as given by the clinical team.
- When and how to contact the Heart Failure Clinic.

## HF-5 Imaging and procedures commonly ordered after discharge

| Procedure code | Description | Prior-auth check |
|---|---|---|
| `CARD-ECHO` | Transthoracic echocardiogram | See PA-001 matrix |
| `CARD-MRI` | Cardiac MRI | See PA-001 matrix |
| `CARD-HOLTER` | 48-hour Holter monitor | See PA-001 matrix |

## HF-6 Escalation

HF-6.1 Any report of chest pain, fainting or severe breathlessness is a clinical emergency and is
escalated immediately to clinical staff (SAFE-001 §SAFE-ESC-01).
