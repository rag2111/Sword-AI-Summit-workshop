# Post-Discharge Follow-up SLA (FU-001)

> **Fictional training document.** Synthetic service levels for a workshop. Not clinical guidance.

## FU-1 Purpose

FU-1.1 Defines the maximum number of days between discharge and the first follow-up appointment,
by condition. These are the network's service-level agreements (SLAs).

## FU-2 General rules

FU-2.1 Required follow-ups are **booked before discharge** and the booking is confirmed with an
appointment ID.

FU-2.2 "Within N days" means the appointment start date is no later than discharge date + N
calendar days.

FU-2.3 If no slot is available inside the window, do not book outside the window without
approval: escalate to the service lead (SAFE-001 §SAFE-ESC-05) and record the escalation.

FU-2.4 If a slot is already taken (booking conflict), choose another slot inside the window.

## FU-3 SLA table

| Condition | Follow-up specialty | Within (days) | Additional requirement |
|---|---|---|---|
| Heart failure (HFrEF) | cardiology | 7 | Primary care within 14 days |
| Heart failure, new diagnosis | cardiology | 7 | Echocardiogram request, check PA (PA-001) |
| COPD exacerbation | pulmonology | 14 | Pulmonary rehab referral before discharge |
| Type 2 diabetes with hyperglycaemia | endocrinology | 14 | Primary care within 14 days |
| Hip fracture, post-surgery | primary-care | 7 | Physiotherapy referral, first session within 7 days |
| Warfarin started or changed | primary-care | 7 | INR check (DP-001 §DP-5.3) |

## FU-4 Measuring the SLA

FU-4.1 The SLA clock starts on the day of discharge (day 0).

FU-4.2 SLA compliance is reported monthly per ward. Target: at least 95 % of discharges meet
FU-3.

## FU-5 Examples (fictional)

FU-5.1 A heart-failure patient discharged on a Monday must have a cardiology appointment no
later than the following Monday.

FU-5.2 A COPD patient discharged on the 1st of the month must see pulmonology by the 15th.
