# Discharge Planning Protocol (LRHN-DP-001)

> **Fictional training document.** Lakeside Regional Health Network does not exist. This protocol was
> written for a hands-on workshop and contains synthetic rules only. It is not clinical guidance and
> must not be used for real patient care.

## DP-1 Purpose and scope

DP-1.1 This protocol defines how care coordinators prepare an adult inpatient for a safe transition
from hospital to home, a community facility or another care setting.

DP-1.2 It applies to all inpatient wards of the network, including Cardiology 4B, Respiratory 2A,
Endocrinology 3C and Orthopaedics 5A.

DP-1.3 Clinical decisions (fitness for discharge, medication changes, diagnoses) remain the
responsibility of the treating clinician. Care coordinators and any digital assistant **coordinate**;
they do not decide clinical matters (see SAFE-001).

## DP-2 Discharge readiness statuses

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `not-started` | No discharge planning activity recorded yet. |
| `pending` | Planning in progress; one or more discharge tasks are open. |
| `ready` | All mandatory tasks closed and the treating clinician has confirmed readiness. |
| `discharged` | The patient has left the ward; post-discharge follow-up rules apply (FU-001). |

DP-2.1 A patient may only move to `ready` when every mandatory task in DP-4 is closed.

## DP-3 Timeline

DP-3.1 Discharge planning starts within **24 hours of admission** (the "day-one rule").

DP-3.2 An estimated date of discharge (EDD) is recorded within 48 hours and reviewed daily.

DP-3.3 Follow-up appointments required by a care pathway must be **booked before the patient leaves
the ward**, not merely requested (see FU-001 §FU-2).

## DP-4 Mandatory discharge tasks

DP-4.1 Medication reconciliation completed and signed by a pharmacist (MED-001).

DP-4.2 Required follow-up appointments booked within the SLA window of the relevant care pathway
(FU-001).

DP-4.3 Specialist referrals created with the correct urgency (REF-001).

DP-4.4 Prior-authorization status checked for every procedure ordered for after discharge
(PA-001). If authorization is required, the request must be submitted before discharge.

DP-4.5 Patient and carer education documented (condition-specific, see the care pathways).

DP-4.6 Transport and home-support needs assessed.

DP-4.7 Discharge summary sent to the patient's primary care practice within 24 hours of discharge.

## DP-5 Complex discharges

DP-5.1 A discharge is "complex" when any of the following apply: age 75 or over living alone,
new home-oxygen requirement, anticoagulation started or changed, more than 10 regular medicines,
or a new diagnosis of heart failure.

DP-5.2 Complex discharges require a multidisciplinary huddle at least 24 hours before the EDD.

DP-5.3 For patients on warfarin, an INR check must be scheduled with primary care within
7 days of discharge.

## DP-6 Hip fracture (post-surgery) discharges

DP-6.1 A physiotherapy referral must be created before discharge and the first physiotherapy
session must take place within **7 days** of discharge.

DP-6.2 A primary care review must be booked within **7 days** of discharge.

DP-6.3 A falls-risk assessment must be documented.

## DP-7 Documentation

DP-7.1 Every booked appointment, referral and prior-authorization check is recorded with its
identifier (appointment ID, referral ID or policy reference).

DP-7.2 When a digital assistant proposes an action, the coordinator records who approved it.
