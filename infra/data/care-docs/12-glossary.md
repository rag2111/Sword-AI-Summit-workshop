# Glossary (GLOS-001)

> **Fictional training document.** Definitions are simplified for a workshop and are not clinical
> guidance.

## GLOS-1 Clinical abbreviations

GLOS-1.1 **COPD** — chronic obstructive pulmonary disease.

GLOS-1.2 **HFrEF** — heart failure with reduced ejection fraction.

GLOS-1.3 **INR** — a blood test used to monitor warfarin; scheduled by primary care.

GLOS-1.4 **HbA1c** — a blood test summarising average glucose over recent months.

## GLOS-2 Coordination terms

GLOS-2.1 **Care pathway** — a standard sequence of coordination steps for a condition, e.g.
CP-HF-001 for heart failure.

GLOS-2.2 **Discharge readiness** — status of discharge planning (`not-started`, `pending`,
`ready`, `discharged`), see DP-001 §DP-2.

GLOS-2.3 **EDD** — estimated date of discharge.

GLOS-2.4 **Referral** — a request for another service to see the patient, with an urgency
(`routine` or `urgent`) and an SLA (REF-001).

GLOS-2.5 **SLA** — service-level agreement; here, the maximum days until a follow-up or a first
contact (FU-001, REF-001).

GLOS-2.6 **Slot** — a bookable appointment time published by the scheduling system, identified by
a slot ID such as `SLOT-CARD-001`.

## GLOS-3 Payer terms

GLOS-3.1 **Payer** — the organisation that pays for care. In this environment: Northwind Health
Plan (NHP), Contoso Care Insurance (CCI) and Fabrikam Mutual Assurance (FMA), all fictional.

GLOS-3.2 **Prior authorization (PA)** — approval a payer requires before some procedures are
scheduled. See PA-001.

GLOS-3.3 **Procedure code** — the network's internal code for an orderable procedure, e.g.
`CARD-ECHO`, `CARD-MRI`, `PULM-REHAB`, `ENDO-CGM`.

## GLOS-4 Data terms

GLOS-4.1 **Synthetic data** — invented data with no link to real people. Everything in this
environment is synthetic.

GLOS-4.2 **Citation** — a reference to a document ID and section ID, e.g. `PA-001 §PA-3`.
